About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Chongqing University.

Research Interests

  • Operator Theory and Operator Algebras
  • Noncommutative Approximation of Hilbert Space Operators
  • Classification and Reducing Subspaces of Operators, particularly Toeplitz Operators

Recently, my research has focused particularly on the reducing structures and classification of Toeplitz operators with non-analytic symbols on the classical Hardy space $H^2(\mathbb D)$.
