About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, Chongqing University.

Research Interests

  • Operator Theory
  • Toeplitz Operators
  • Operator Algebras
  • Reducing Subspaces of Operators

Recently, my research focuses particularly on the reducing structures and classification of Toeplitz operators with non-analytic symbols on the classical Hardy space $H^2(\mathbb D)$.
